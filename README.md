### Overview & Focus

Focused on **Applied Statistics, Actuarial Mathematics, Quantitative Trading, and Machine Learning**. Experienced in dual-alpha futures execution architectures, multi-criteria decision making (Best-Worst Method), credit risk scoring, deep learning pipelines, and computational statistical programming in R and Python.

- **Trainee / Intern** at *OJK Institute (OJKI)*, Directorate of Human Capital Competency Development.
- **Undergraduate Statistics Student** at *Universitas Diponegoro* (Faculty of Science and Mathematics).
- **Quantitative Strategist & Developer** behind *AzuraAzureQuant* (Dual-Alpha Futures Trading System).
- **Lead Researcher & Author** for the *ESG-Integrated Bimodal Credit Scoring (ESG-BCS)* framework for OJK Innovation Paper 2026.
